Frequently Asked Questions

Solar Rooftop & Subsidy FAQs

Clear, transparent answers to help you make an informed clean energy investment.

What is the subsidy amount under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Under the Central Government's PM Surya Ghar scheme, residential homeowners receive a fixed financial subsidy of ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or higher capacity. The subsidy amount is credited directly into your bank account via DBT following JBVNL net-meter commissioning.

How does grid-connected net-metering work in Jharkhand?

A bidirectional net-meter records solar electricity exported to the grid when your panels produce surplus energy during the day, and records grid power consumed at night. You are billed only for the net difference, reducing your monthly electricity bill by up to 90%.

How much rooftop space is required for a solar installation?

Each 1 kWp of solar panel capacity requires approximately 100 square feet (9.3 sq.m) of shadow-free rooftop space. For example, a 3 kW residential rooftop installation requires roughly 300 sq.ft of area.

What is the expected lifespan and warranty of the system?

Tier-1 monocrystalline solar panels have an operational lifespan of 25 to 30 years and come with a 25-year linear performance warranty guaranteeing >= 80% output efficiency at Year 25. String inverters carry 5 to 10-year manufacturer warranties.

Will solar panels work during power cuts or cloudy days?

On cloudy days, panels continue generating roughly 25% to 40% of their rated output via diffuse light. For power cuts, standard on-grid systems shut off for grid safety (anti-islanding), while hybrid systems with battery storage or DG synchronization maintain continuous power.
