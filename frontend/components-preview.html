5. Accessible FAQ Accordion

What is the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy amount?

Under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, residential homeowners receive ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or higher capacity. The subsidy is credited directly via DBT to your bank account after net-meter commissioning.

How does grid-connected solar net-metering work with JBVNL?

A bidirectional net-meter records electricity exported to the grid when your solar panels produce more than you consume, and imports grid power at night. You are billed only for the net difference.
