REGULATORY RIGOR

Tender Compliance & State Nodal Liaisoning

1. 100% DCR & ALMM Compliance

All panels and equipment strictly comply with the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM).

2. State Nodal Agency Approvals

Direct liaisoning with JREDA (Jharkhand), BREDA (Bihar), and WBREDA (West Bengal) for fast regulatory clearance and project commissioning.

3. Comprehensive O&M Guarantees

Turnkey 5-year Comprehensive Maintenance Contracts (CMC) covering routine health checks, remote monitoring, and performance warranties.
