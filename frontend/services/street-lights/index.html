ROBUST OUTDOOR ENGINEERING

Autonomous Solar Illumination

1. All-in-One Integrated Design

Compact luminaire integrating mono solar panel, MPPT charge controller, motion sensor, and lithium battery inside a weatherproof IP65 aluminum casing.

2. LiFePO4 Lithium Battery

High-density Lithium Ferro-Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery chemistry provides over 2,000 charge-discharge cycles with exceptional heat resistance.

3. Smart Dusk-to-Dawn Automation

Automated optical sensing activates lighting at sunset and turns off at sunrise, with intelligent dimming to preserve battery during cloudy weather.
