About JDC Solar

Powering a Sustainable Future with Smart Solar Solutions

JDC Solar is a professional solar Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) company committed to delivering reliable, high-performance solar power plants for residential, commercial, industrial, and government clients across India.

Jagatdhan Group Lineage
Adityapur HQ, Jamshedpur
500+ Commissioned Projects
CORPORATE HERITAGE

Engineering Excellence Rooted in Industrial Trust

As a dedicated clean energy venture of Jagatdhan Commodities Pvt. Ltd., JDC Solar operates from its engineering headquarters in the Adityapur Industrial Area, Jamshedpur. We specialize in end-to-end solar solutions—from initial shadow analysis and electrical design to net-metering approvals, structural installation, and long-term operations & maintenance.

We empower homeowners to eliminate their electricity bills through the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, while enabling industrial manufacturers and commercial establishments to achieve substantial operational savings and 40% Section 32 tax depreciation benefits.
