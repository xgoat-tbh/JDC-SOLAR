Section 32 Accelerated Depreciation: 40% Year-1 Tax Shield for Businesses

While direct PM Surya Ghar DBT subsidies apply to domestic consumers, Indian enterprises, manufacturing plants, and hospitals can claim 40% Accelerated Depreciation (AD) under the Income Tax Act to write off substantial solar capital expenditures.

1. Understanding Section 32 of the Income Tax Act

Under Section 32(1)(ii) of the Indian Income Tax Act, solar photovoltaic energy systems are classified as eligible renewable energy devices carrying an accelerated depreciation rate of 40% on a Written Down Value (WDV) basis in the first year of commissioning.

This tax provision allows profitable commercial enterprises, healthcare institutions, and industrial factories to write off 40% of the entire turnkey solar project value against taxable operating profits in Year 1, yielding an immediate cash-flow tax shield.

2. The "180 Days" Commissioning Rule

Under Indian tax guidelines:

  • Commissioned before September 30th (> 180 Days): The company is eligible to claim the full 40% depreciation in the current financial year.
  • Commissioned after September 30th (< 180 Days): The company can claim 20% depreciation in the first year, with the remaining 20% carrying over into subsequent financial years.

3. Illustrative Financial Model (100 kWp Plant)

Sample 100 kWp Industrial Rooftop Installation:

  • Turnkey Project Cost: ₹ 50,00,000 (@ ₹50,000 / kW)
  • Year 1 Depreciation (40%): ₹ 20,00,000
  • Direct Tax Savings (at 25% Corporate Tax): ₹ 5,00,000 in Year 1
  • Annual Power Bill Savings: ₹ 12,41,000 / year (1,46,000 units @ ₹8.50/unit)
  • Combined Year 1 Cash Flow Benefit: ₹ 17,41,000
  • Effective Payback Period: ~2.8 to 3.2 Years

4. Statutory Documentation Required

To claim Section 32 depreciation with your statutory tax auditor, JDC Solar provides a certified project handover package:

  • JBVNL / TSUISL Net-Metering Work Completion & Commissioning Certificate
  • Itemized GST Tax Invoice with component breakdown (Solar PV modules & inverters)
  • Chartered Engineer's Plant Handover Certificate
