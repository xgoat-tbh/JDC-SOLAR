PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Complete Subsidy Guide

The Government of India provides up to ₹78,000 direct financial subsidy to homeowners installing grid-connected rooftop solar. As an approved EPC vendor in Jharkhand, JDC Solar manages 100% of your National Portal paperwork, JBVNL approvals, and net-meter commissioning.

Official Sizing Rule: Systems above 3 kWp receive the maximum capped subsidy of ₹78,000 for individual residential meters. Commercial, industrial, and institutional connections receive ₹0 direct subsidy but are eligible for 40% Section 32 Accelerated Tax Depreciation.
WHO QUALIFIES

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Eligibility Criteria

Check if your property meets the official Ministry requirements.

1. Residential Electricity Meter

The applicant must have an active residential grid connection with a local DISCOM (such as JBVNL or TSUISL in Jharkhand) in their name.

2. Roof Ownership & Space

The applicant must own an unobstructed, shadow-free rooftop space (~100 sq.ft per kWp capacity) suitable for solar structure mounting.

3. DCR & ALMM Certified Panels

To qualify for central DBT subsidy, installations must use 100% Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) solar cells and ALMM-approved modules.

PORTAL APPLICATION WORKFLOW

How to Claim Your ₹78,000 Subsidy Step-by-Step

JDC Solar guides you through every milestone on the National Portal.

01

Portal Registration

Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in using your JBVNL Consumer Number and mobile.

02

Feasibility Approval

JBVNL reviews your sanctioned load and approves the technical grid connection feasibility.

03

JDC Solar EPC

Select JDC Solar as your approved vendor. Our engineers install Tier-1 DCR panels and inverters.

04

Net-Meter & DBT

DISCOM installs the bidirectional meter, conducts inspection, and ₹78k subsidy is credited to your bank.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Mandatory Documents for Subsidy Registration

Have these 4 items ready for quick national portal verification.

1. Latest Electricity Bill

Recent JBVNL or TSUISL electricity bill showing consumer number, sanctioned load, and address.

2. Aadhaar Card

Aadhaar card of the electricity connection owner (must be linked to bank account for DBT credit).

3. Cancelled Cheque / Bank Passbook

Bank details showing account number and IFSC code for direct DBT subsidy deposit.

4. Clear Rooftop Photograph

Clear photo of the roof area where solar panels will be installed (taken during site survey).

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PM Surya Ghar Scheme FAQs

Is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy deducted upfront from my invoice?

No. Under the official PM Surya Ghar guidelines, the subsidy is paid as a Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into your linked bank account from the central government within 30 days of net-meter commissioning.

Can I claim the subsidy if the electricity meter is in my landlord's name?

No. The electricity bill, bank account, and Aadhaar card must belong to the same person. If you own the property, you can apply for a name transfer with JBVNL before registering on the National Portal.

What happens if my sanctioned load is lower than my desired solar capacity?

Your rooftop solar capacity cannot exceed your sanctioned load with JBVNL. JDC Solar assists you in submitting a load enhancement application to JBVNL alongside your solar application.
