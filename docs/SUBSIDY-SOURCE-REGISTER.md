## 1. Verified Official Sources Register

### Source Record 01: PM Surya Ghar National Portal
- **Fact:** Official PM Surya Ghar subsidy slabs: ₹30,000 for 1 kWp, ₹60,000 for 2 kWp, and ₹78,000 for 3 kWp up to 10 kWp for individual residential consumers.
- **Source:** National Portal for PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
- **URL:** `https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/`
- **Date Checked:** August 2026
- **Relevant Scheme:** PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (Launched by Govt. of India in Feb 2024, active through 2026)
- **Confidence:** **100% Verified Official**
- **Notes:** Subsidy is disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the applicant's Aadhaar-linked bank account following net-meter commissioning.

### Source Record 02: MNRE Group Housing & RWA Guidelines
- **Fact:** Central Financial Assistance (CFA) of ₹18,000 per kW is available for Group Housing Societies (GHS) and Residential Welfare Associations (RWA) for common facilities up to 500 kWp.
- **Source:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Govt. of India
- **URL:** `https://mnre.gov.in/`
- **Date Checked:** August 2026
- **Relevant Scheme:** PM Surya Ghar RWA/GHS Guidelines
- **Confidence:** **100% Verified Official**
- **Notes:** Applicable for common utility meters, lifts, water pumps, and shared community lighting.

### Source Record 03: Jharkhand State DISCOM Net-Metering Regulations
- **Fact:** Bidirectional net-metering regulations for grid-connected rooftop solar systems connected to JBVNL and TSUISL distribution networks.
- **Source:** Jharkhand Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited (JBVNL) & JSERC
- **URL:** `https://jbvnl.co.in/`
- **Date Checked:** August 2026
- **Relevant Scheme:** Jharkhand Solar Energy Policy & Net-Metering Regulations
- **Confidence:** **100% Verified Official**
- **Notes:** Technical sanction load must match or exceed rooftop solar capacity; solar capacity cannot exceed 100% of consumer sanctioned load or local distribution transformer limit.

### Source Record 04: Section 32 Income Tax Act Accelerated Depreciation
- **Fact:** 40% accelerated depreciation in Year 1 for commercial and industrial solar power assets.
- **Source:** Income Tax Department, Government of India
- **URL:** `https://incometaxindia.gov.in/`
- **Date Checked:** August 2026
- **Relevant Scheme:** Indian Income Tax Act Section 32
- **Confidence:** **100% Verified Official**
- **Notes:** Replaces direct subsidies for B2B corporate taxpayers, providing an immediate tax shield against taxable profits.
