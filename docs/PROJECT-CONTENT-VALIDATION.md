## 3. Data Integrity & Anti-Hallucination Compliance

1. **Deterministic Sizing:** System sizing and generation figures strictly follow the engineering baseline: $\text{Annual Generation} = kW_p \times 1,460\text{ kWh/year}$ ($4.0\text{ kWh/kWp/day}$ in Eastern India).
2. **Deterministic Financials:** Commercial savings calculated at JBVNL commercial tariff ($\text{₹}8.50/\text{kWh}$), residential savings at domestic slab ($\text{₹}6.50/\text{kWh}$).
3. **Anonymized Protection:** Corporate and healthcare client names are kept in industry-category format until formal marketing clearance is provided by JDC management.
