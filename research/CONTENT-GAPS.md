## 1. Executive Summary of Content Gaps

While JDC Solar possesses established corporate credentials (a Jagatdhan Commodities Pvt. Ltd. company, 500+ clients, Khetan partnership), the live website omits critical decision-making data that residential homeowners, factory managers, and government procurement officers expect before contacting a solar EPC company.

---

## 2. Granular Content Gaps by Category

---

### Gap 1: Outdated & Incomplete Subsidy Information (PM Surya Ghar)
- **Current State:** The homepage highlights "PM Surya Ghar Yojana" and "Kusum Yojana" with brief 1-sentence summaries, but provides **zero details on subsidy amounts, eligibility criteria, required documents, or application procedures**.
- **Information Missing:**
  - Standard national subsidy benchmarks: ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW+ systems.
  - State-specific additional top-up subsidies (e.g. Jharkhand / Bihar state incentives).
  - Step-by-step consumer onboarding workflow: National Portal registration, DISCOM feasibility approval, vendor selection, net-meter installation, subsidy disbursement directly into bank account.
- **Buyer Impact:** High friction. Prospective customers must leave the website to search Google for PM Surya Ghar details.

---

### Gap 2: Solar Calculator Logic Disconnects
- **Current State:** The calculator includes dropdowns for 15 Indian states and Connected Load, but the underlying JavaScript **completely ignores the selected state and connected load**!
- **Information Missing:**
  - State-specific electricity tariffs (e.g., JBVNL domestic tariff of ₹6.25/unit vs MSEDCL Maharashtra tariff of ₹8.50/unit).
  - Roof area requirement calculation (approx. 100 sq.ft per 1 kW of solar panels).
  - Monthly savings breakdown vs annual payback schedule.
  - Financial return metrics (IRR / Simple Payback in years).

---

### Gap 3: Complete Absence of Project Case Studies & Technical Specifications
- **Current State:** The Projects page lists 6 generic titles with 1-sentence descriptions and no photos, specs, or client names.
- **Information Missing:**
  - Exact system capacities (kWp / MWp).
  - Equipment datasheets (Tier-1 Mono PERC / TOPCon bifacial panel types, DCR vs Non-DCR modules, string vs microinverters).
  - Site photos, drone imagery, generation logs, and client testimonials.
  - Actual return-on-investment numbers and monthly electricity bill savings achieved.

---

### Gap 4: Commercial & Industrial (B2B) Financial Models
- **Current State:** High-level mention of industrial rooftop solar with zero commercial details.
- **Information Missing:**
  - CAPEX (Direct Ownership) vs OPEX / RESCO (Zero-Investment Power Purchase Agreement) models.
  - Section 32 Accelerated Depreciation (40% tax benefit) calculation guide for Indian businesses.
  - Net metering vs Gross metering vs Open Access regulations for High Tension (HT) industrial consumers.

---

### Gap 5: Warranty, Guarantees & O&M Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- **Current State:** Homepage mentions "lifespan of 25 years or more", but does not outline formal warranty coverage.
- **Information Missing:**
  - 25-30 Year Linear Power Output Performance Warranty for Solar Panels.
  - 5-10 Year Product Warranty for Inverters.
  - 5 Year System Workmanship Warranty by JDC Solar.
  - O&M Service Level Agreement response times (e.g., 24-48 hour on-site technician dispatch in Jharkhand/Bihar).
